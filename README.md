## The Idea Behind NEXTSTEP

During my first year of college, I found myself lost. I didn't know where to begin or what I should start with.

There were so many choices: YouTube, Google platforms, online courses, offline courses, and different learning paths.
Everyone seemed to recommend a different subject or a different starting point. Sometimes,these recommendations were even 
completely different from what I was learning in college.

The more resources I found, the more confused I became.

That is why I created **NEXTSTEP**.

NEXTSTEP is a guide that helps you decide where to start based on what you want to become. You choose the career path you are interested in,
and NEXTSTEP guides you through the skills you need to learn along the way.

It starts with the **basic skills** you need to build a strong foundation, then moves on to more **advanced skills** as you progress.

NEXTSTEP also provides learning resources from real platforms that already exist and offer courses or materials related to each specific skill or subject. 
This means you don't have to search everywhere wondering, "Where should I start?" or "What should I learn next?"

But NEXTSTEP is not only about skills and resources.

It also has a **community**, because learning can sometimes feel lonely. 
When you see other people who are learning the same things or are at a similar point in their journey, you realize that you are not alone.

That sense of belonging can give you the motivation and strength to keep going.
Sometimes, the most important thing a learner needs to tell themselves is simply:
> **"I'm not alone."**

And that is the idea behind **NEXTSTEP**.
